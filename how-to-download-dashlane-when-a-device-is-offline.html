Common Problems and Solutions

If the sign in page repeatedly reloads, verify your connection, close the browser, reopen it, and retry in a normal window. If the problem persists, try a different trusted network or a supported browser to isolate the cause.

If an extension is missing or disabled, open the browser’s extension management page and confirm it is installed and enabled. Check whether another extension or browser policy is blocking it, especially on work or school managed devices.

If the app appears to freeze, wait for any ongoing update to finish, then restart the app and device. Avoid repeatedly uninstalling until you have confirmed that your account data is available and your recovery options are understood.

If autofill fails on one website, inspect whether the login form is unusual or whether the stored credential matches the current website address. Try selecting the correct entry manually instead of changing multiple security settings at once.

If a subscription is not recognized, sign in with the email address used for the purchase and inspect the billing or account status. A receipt or order confirmation may help you identify the correct account, but never post it publicly.

If a verification prompt does not arrive, confirm that the registered email or authentication device is available. Check filtered email folders and device time settings. Repeated attempts can trigger temporary restrictions, so wait when prompted.

Security and Privacy Tips

Keep your operating system, browser, and password manager updated because updates may contain compatibility improvements and security fixes. Install only from trusted sources and be cautious of search ads imitating download pages.

Use unique passwords for important accounts and enable supported multifactor authentication where appropriate. Store recovery information securely and separately from the device most likely to be lost or damaged.

Never give a support agent remote control of your computer merely to demonstrate a login issue. If remote assistance is ever considered, verify the provider independently, understand the access requested, and protect your sensitive data.

When troubleshooting, capture the error text rather than a screenshot containing personal credentials. Remove email addresses, payment information, and security codes before sharing a diagnostic image.
